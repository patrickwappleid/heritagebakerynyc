Ancient Grains

Three Things
We Believe

From biodynamic soil in Europe to a Brooklyn table — our commitment to every step of the journey.

01

Biodynamic Farming

Sourced exclusively from certified biodynamic farms in Belgium & France, where soil is treated as a living ecosystem — no synthetic inputs, ever.

02

Sustainable Shipping

Every shipment crosses the Atlantic by sailboat — wind-powered, carbon-zero. Slow, deliberate, and honest.

03

Milled Onsite

Fine granite mills in our Brooklyn bakery turn whole grains into flour fresh before every bake. Nothing stripped, nothing lost.
